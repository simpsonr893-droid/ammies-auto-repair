# Colorado Leads

`colorado-leads.csv` — 100 local service businesses in Colorado that already have websites,
compiled via web research (September 2026). These are prospects for the ShopLine AI agency
pitch (website redesign, AI chatbot, SEO — see `/pitch.html`).

## Columns

- `id` — row number 1–100
- `company` — business name
- `category` — service vertical
- `city`, `state` — primary service area found in research
- `website` — the company's site (verify before outreach; URLs were found via search results)
- `notes` — quick context for personalizing outreach (years in business, specialties)

## Notes

- National franchises (Orkin, Terminix, TruGreen, Molly Maid, Two Men and a Truck, etc.)
  were excluded in favor of locally owned/operated businesses.
- Verify each website and gather contact emails/phones before running outreach.
