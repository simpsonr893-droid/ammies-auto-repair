Straight Answers

The questions every owner asks.

Will it sound like a robot and annoy my customers?

No — try the demo and judge for yourself. It's trained to talk like a good service writer: plain language, real empathy for someone who just got rear-ended, and straight answers. Customers regularly don't realize they weren't texting a person.

What happens when it can't answer something?

It never bluffs. For anything outside what it knows — a firm price quote, a complex claim dispute — it takes the customer's details, promises a callback, and sends the full conversation to your phone immediately. You lose nothing; you just get the lead instead of a hang-up.

Do I have to change my phone number or website?

No. The receptionist installs on the website you already have with one small snippet, and missed-call text-back works with your existing phone line. Nothing about how you operate changes.

I already have a front-desk person. Why would I need this?

Keep them — this covers the hours they can't. Nights, weekends, sick days, and the 11 AM rush when the phone rings while they're checking a customer out. It's coverage, not replacement.

How long does setup take?

Under a week. You give us 30 minutes to walk through your services and schedule; we build, train, and test the receptionist and hand you a preview before anything goes live.

What if I want to cancel?

It's month-to-month with no contract. Cancel with an email, effective at the end of your billing cycle — and everything captured up to that point stays yours.
