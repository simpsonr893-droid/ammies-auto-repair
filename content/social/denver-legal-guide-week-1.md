## Day 4 — Thursday · Know Your Rights

**Theme:** After a car accident in Colorado — 4 things to know
**Platforms:** Instagram, Facebook, X
**Best time:** 11:30 AM MT

**Hook:** Rear-ended on I-25? Here's what Colorado law actually gives you time to do.

**Caption:**
Know Your Rights Thursday 🏛️ If you're in a car accident in Colorado:

🚗 You generally have 3 YEARS to file a claim for car accident injuries in Colorado — but evidence disappears fast, so don't wait *(verify current statute before relying on it)*
🚗 You must report accidents involving injury or damage — leaving the scene is a crime
🚗 The first settlement offer is rarely the best one — you're allowed to say "let me review this"
🚗 Colorado uses modified comparative negligence — you can still recover if you were partly (less than 50%) at fault

None of this is legal advice — but a 30-minute consult with a Denver injury attorney usually is free.
